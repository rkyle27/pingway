Privacy Policy for Pingway

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Pingway respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any data associated with your use of the bot. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.
1. Information We Collect

To provide core features such as personal timers, notifications, and custom settings, Pingway collects and stores limited data:
    Discord IDs: Unique numerical IDs associated with Discord users (e.g., user IDs for managing personal timers and direct message settings) and Discord servers (guilds) where the bot is installed.
    User Preferences: Custom configurations set within the bot, such as notification toggles (notify_dm) and timestamp logs (next_celebration_timestamp).
    Interaction Data: Information required to process commands and deliver responses (such as slash command inputs).

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected by Pingway is used strictly for the following purposes:
    To operate, maintain, and provide the core functionality of the bot (such as sending scheduled direct message reminders and managing countdowns).
    To save your personal preferences across sessions.
    To troubleshoot bugs, ensure system stability, and improve bot performance.

3. Data Storage and Security
    Hosting: Pingway is hosted on secure external cloud servers (such as Cybrancee), and data is stored in a secure database.
    Security Measures: Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect your stored data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no internet transmission or database storage is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
    We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties.
    Data is only accessed when necessary to maintain the bot's infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion
    Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the bot's features to you or your server.
    If you wish to have your personal data completely removed from our database, you can request data deletion by contacting the developer or removing the bot and requesting a data purge.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Pingway after updates constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

7. Contact
    If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, you can contact the developer directly through Discord.
